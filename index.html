Design Thinking Project

Campus CleanTrack

A smart waste management system that tells cleaning staff when a campus bin is almost full, so bins are emptied on time.

Try the Dashboard

Note: This website uses simulated readings. It is a prototype demo, not live data.

The Problem

Overflowing bins

On campus, bins in corridors, canteens and hostels can fill up before anyone notices.

Fixed cleaning rounds

Staff often empty bins on a fixed schedule. Some bins are still half empty, while others are already overflowing.

Mixed waste

When bins are full, people drop waste nearby or in the wrong bin. This makes recycling and composting harder.

Proposed Solution

Each bin has an ultrasonic sensor that measures how full it is. An Arduino reads the sensor and turns on LEDs. At 80% it also sounds a buzzer. Staff can then act before the bin overflows.

Objectives

  • Monitor the fill level of wet and dry waste bins.
  • Give an early warning at 80% capacity.
  • Reduce overflow and bad smell.
  • Build a low-cost prototype using simple parts.

Benefits

  • Bins are emptied when needed, not only by schedule.
  • A cleaner and more hygienic campus.
  • Staff time can be used better.
  • Easy to understand and to expand.

Important: The sensors only detect how full a bin is. They do not detect the type of waste. Users must separate wet and dry waste by themselves.

Interactive Dashboard

All readings here are simulated. Move the sliders or pick a demo scenario.

Demo scenarios:

Status: below 50% = Normal (green LED) · 50% to 79% = Filling (yellow LED) · 80% and above = Full, alert (red LED and buzzer).

How It Works

  1. Measure: The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, placed under the lid, sends sound waves down. It measures the time for the echo to return.
  2. Calculate: The Arduino converts the time to distance. A short distance means the waste is close to the lid, so the bin is fuller.
  3. Compare: The Arduino compares the fill level with set limits (50% and 80%).
  4. Signal: It lights a green, yellow or red LED. At 80% or more the buzzer also sounds.
  5. Act: Cleaning staff see the red LED or hear the buzzer and empty the bin.

Components, Limitations and Future Work

Components

  • Arduino Uno: the controller (brain).
  • Ultrasonic sensor (HC-SR04), 2 pcs: one per bin, measures distance.
  • LEDs (green, yellow, red): show the status.
  • Buzzer: sounds at 80% or more.
  • Resistors, jumper wires, breadboard, USB power: basic circuit parts.

Limitations

  • It cannot detect the type of waste.
  • Uneven piles can give a wrong fill reading.
  • Very soft or angled items may reflect sound poorly.
  • It works only near the bin. There is no remote alert yet.
  • This website shows simulated values only.

Future Improvements

  • Add Wi-Fi (for example, ESP8266 or ESP32) to send data to a live dashboard.
  • Send SMS or app alerts to staff.
  • Use several sensors per bin for better accuracy.
  • Add solar power for outdoor bins.
  • Plan cleaning routes using collected data.
