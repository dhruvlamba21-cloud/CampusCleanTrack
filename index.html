The Problem

Full bins cause
real trouble

Bins in corridors, canteens and hostels can fill up before anyone notices.

Overflowing bins

Waste spills out, smells bad and attracts pests.

Fixed rounds

Staff clean on a schedule. Some bins are half empty, others are overflowing.

Mixed waste

When bins are full, people leave waste nearby or use the wrong bin.

No early warning

Nobody knows a bin is nearly full until it is too late.

Solution

A simple smart bin
that gives early alerts

An ultrasonic sensor measures how full each bin is. An Arduino shows the status with LEDs, and a buzzer sounds at 80%.

Objectives

  • Monitor the fill level of wet and dry bins.
  • Warn staff at 80% capacity.
  • Reduce overflow and bad smell.
  • Build a low-cost prototype.

Benefits

  • Bins are emptied when needed.
  • A cleaner, more hygienic campus.
  • Better use of staff time.
  • Easy to understand and expand.

Workflow

How the system
works

01

Measure

The HC-SR04 sensor under the lid sends sound waves down and times the echo.

02

Calculate

The Arduino turns the echo time into distance. Shorter distance means a fuller bin.

03

Compare

It checks the fill level against the 50% and 80% limits.

04

Signal

A green, yellow or red LED lights up. At 80% the buzzer also sounds.

05

Act

Staff see the red LED or hear the buzzer and empty the bin.

Build

Components, limits
and next steps

Components

  • Arduino Uno: the controller.
  • HC-SR04 sensors (2): measure distance.
  • LEDs: green, yellow, red status.
  • Buzzer: sounds at 80% or more.
  • Resistors, wires, breadboard, USB power.

Limitations

  • Cannot detect the type of waste.
  • Uneven piles can give a wrong reading.
  • Soft or angled items may reflect sound poorly.
  • No remote alerts yet.
  • This website uses simulated values only.

Future Improvements

  • Add Wi-Fi (ESP8266 or ESP32) for a live dashboard.
  • Send SMS or app alerts.
  • Use more sensors per bin.
  • Add solar power for outdoor bins.
  • Plan cleaning routes from the data.
FAQ

Your questions
answered

Does the system sort waste?

No. It only measures fill level. People must put wet and dry waste in the correct bins.

Is the data on this site real?

No. All readings are simulated to demonstrate the idea.

Why use an ultrasonic sensor?

It is cheap, easy to use with Arduino, and measures distance without touching the waste.

What happens at 80%?

The red LED turns on, the buzzer sounds and the dashboard shows an alert.
